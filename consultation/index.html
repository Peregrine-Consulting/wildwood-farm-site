Consultation

Finding the program that fits you.

Recovery isn't one thing, and the right support depends on where you actually are. The first conversation is free, and it is not a sales call.

What we'll talk about

  • Where things stand right now, in your words.
  • What has already been tried, and what about it did or didn't work.
  • What kind of structure and clinical support would actually help.
  • Whether Wildwood is the right fit, or whether something else would serve you better.

You can ask what a day looks like, whether we've worked with someone in your situation, or what happens if things don't go to plan. You don't have to be certain, or ready, or sober to have the conversation.

If we're not the right fit

We'd rather tell you that than admit you. Extended sober living on a working farm suits some people extremely well and others not at all, and six months away is a serious thing to take on. If a different level of care or a different setting makes more sense, we'll say so and help you think through where to look.

How to reach us

Call or text 203-800-6159. If a parent, sibling or partner is making the first call, that's common and welcome.

Call 203-800-6159 I'm asking for someone else

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available day and night by calling or texting 988.
