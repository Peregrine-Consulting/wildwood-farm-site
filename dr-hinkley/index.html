Clinical leadership

Dr. Michael Hinkley

Founder and clinical director. A licensed therapist whose work centres on identity, shame, addiction and trauma.

Why the clinician lives on site

Most sober living is run by house managers. Rules get enforced, rent gets collected, and the clinical work happens somewhere else, with someone who never sees how a resident actually lives.

Wildwood was built the other way round. Michael is on the farm, in the groups, and in the ordinary business of the week. When something surfaces on a Tuesday afternoon, it gets worked with on a Tuesday afternoon rather than saved for an appointment.

The work he does

Michael's clinical focus is the overlap between addiction and shame: what happens to people who learned early that some part of them was unacceptable, and who found something that made that easier to carry.

For a great many queer people, that is the whole shape of the story. Treating the substance without treating the shame underneath tends to buy a few months. Treating both is slower, and it holds.

What working with him is like

Direct, and not especially interested in performance. He will tell you plainly if he thinks Wildwood is the wrong fit, which is the same standard he applies to the first phone call.
