The family programme

The Wildwood Invitational Family Intervention brings families into the weekly work. Not to assign blame, and not to stage a confrontation, but to build a clearer map of what this family has survived, where the patterns formed, and what support can realistically look like afterward.

It is invitational by design. Nobody is ambushed, and nobody is required to perform remorse. The work is structured, facilitated, and paced so that people can stay in the room.

What families usually want to know

Can we be involved if the relationship is strained?

Usually yes, and strained is the norm rather than the exception. The programme is designed for families who have already had the difficult years, not for families who are getting along.

What if we're the reason some of this happened?

That comes up often, in one form or another. The work is not an exercise in assigning fault. It is an attempt to understand how a family arrived where it is, which is the only useful starting point for changing anything.

What if they won't talk to us?

Then we start where things actually are. Sometimes the first months are about the resident's own work, and family sessions come later. We'll be honest with you about the pace.

Can I call before they've agreed to anything?

Yes. Many first conversations are with a parent, sibling or partner rather than the person who would be living here.
